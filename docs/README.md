# Genesis 2026: Ideathon
# Integrated Renewable Energy Microgrid

**Team:** Hustlers  
**Event:** Genesis Ideathon  
**Domain:** Energy, Mobility and Smart Infrastructure

## Introduction

Our project proposes a hybrid renewable energy microgrid
for rural electrification. It combines solar, micro-hydro
and biogas generation with battery storage, biodiesel
backup and smart energy management.

## Problem Statement

Rural communities face unreliable electricity, frequent
power outages and high fuel costs. These problems affect
households, agriculture, schools, healthcare and businesses.

## Objectives

- Improve the reliability of rural electricity supply.
- Use locally available renewable energy resources.
- Reduce dependence on conventional diesel generators.
- Coordinate electricity generation and battery storage.
- Evaluate the technical and economic feasibility of the system.

## Proposed Components

- **Solar panels:** Generate electricity from sunlight.
- **Micro-hydro system:** Generate electricity where sufficient
  water flow and usable head are available.
- **Biogas generator:** Convert biogas from organic waste into electricity.
- **Battery storage:** Store surplus electricity for later use.
- **Biodiesel generator:** Provide backup when other sources are insufficient.
- **Smart controller:** Coordinate generation, storage and demand.
- **Inverters and protection equipment:** Support electricity conversion
  and safe distribution.

## Proposed Working Process

1. Generate electricity using available renewable resources.
2. Supply electricity to local consumers.
3. Store surplus electricity in batteries.
4. Use stored energy and controlled generator operation
   when renewable generation cannot meet demand.

## Planned Tools

- **MATLAB/Simulink:** Model and simulate system performance.
- **HOMER Pro:** Evaluate component sizing, costs and feasibility.

## Expected Outcomes

- More reliable electricity for rural communities.
- Reduced conventional diesel consumption.
- Better use of local renewable resources.
- Improved electricity access for essential services.
